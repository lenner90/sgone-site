Privacy Policy

SGOne: Bus, MRT & Parking · Last updated 25 November 2026

SGOne shows live transport, parking and weather information for Singapore. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: no sign-up, no ads, no tracking, and we don't keep a profile of you.

What stays on your phone

  • Your Home and Work places.
  • Your saved bus stops, buses, MRT stations and carparks.
  • Your Today layout and language setting.

Deleting the app deletes them.

What is sent to our server

To show you live information, SGOne sends a request to our server, which looks up the answer and sends it back. Depending on the screen, a request can include:

  • Your location, while the app is open and only if you allow it, to find nearby bus stops, stations, carparks and the weather where you are.
  • Start and end points, such as the coordinates of Home and Work, to work out your drive time and public transport route.
  • What you type in search, such as a postal code, bus number or carpark name.

We use this only to answer that request. We don't store it in a database, build a history of where you go, or link it to your name. Our hosting provider's request logs may keep these details for a short time (usually a few days) for security and troubleshooting, and then delete them.

Anonymous account

When you first open SGOne, it creates an anonymous account with a random ID so our server can tell genuine app requests apart from abuse. It has no name, email or phone number attached.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our server.
  • OneMap (Singapore Land Authority) handles address search and public transport routes.
  • Google Routes calculates drive times. It receives the start and end points of the trip.
  • LTA DataMall and data.gov.sg provide bus, MRT, carpark, checkpoint and weather data. Our server fetches this data on its own; your details are not sent to them.

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

SGOne has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell or share your information.

Location permission

SGOne only asks for location while you use the app. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings; the app still works if you search for places instead.

Children

SGOne does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
