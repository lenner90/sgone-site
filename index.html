SGOne

Bus, MRT, parking and weather for Singapore

SGOne puts what you check before leaving the house on one screen: live bus arrivals, MRT status, free carpark lots, rain and haze, and Causeway and 2nd Link traffic. It is free, with no sign-up and no ads.

Contact

Bus times or weather don't show

Pull down to refresh. If nearby stops, carparks or weather stay empty, check that Location is allowed for SGOne in your phone's settings, and that you have a data connection.

Save your stops and stations

Tap the star next to a bus or MRT station to see it on your Today screen. Open More → Your places to set Home and Work for your morning plan and drive time.

Choose what Today shows

On the Today screen, open Customise to add, remove or reorder cards. Pick Bus & MRT, Driver, or both.

Chinese / 中文

Open More → Language / 语言 to switch between English and 中文, or follow your phone's setting.

Where does the data come from?

Bus, MRT and carpark data come from LTA DataMall. Weather, PSI and UV come from NEA via data.gov.sg. Maps and address search come from OneMap by the Singapore Land Authority. Times are estimates and may differ from what happens on the road. SGOne is not affiliated with any government agency.

Is my data private?

SGOne has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your Home and Work stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
